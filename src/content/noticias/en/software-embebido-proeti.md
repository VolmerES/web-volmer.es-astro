Since June 2026 I have been working at **PROETI**, a materials testing machine company, as an embedded software developer.

I work on the machines' control system (compression, tension, CBR): the microcontroller firmware, the C service that drives the machine on embedded ARM Linux, and the machine interface.

It is the C and low-level work I learned at 42 Madrid, applied every day to real machines that cannot afford to fail. I bring that same care to Volmer Studio's websites and apps, which carries on as always: I handle the projects and the maintenance myself.
