In January I took part in the tenth HackJam by **Madrid in Game**, Madrid City Council's initiative to grow the video game industry. This time the challenge came from the **EMT**, Madrid's municipal bus company: make games about getting around the city, in 48 hours, at **42 Madrid Fundación Telefónica**.

There were 112 participants in 20 teams. Ours entered **Next Stop: Madrid** in the "Exploring the City by bus" category, and won.

## The game

You drive a bus through Madrid: dodge cars, pick up passengers and pass the city's landmarks to get as far as you can. Every place you pass is saved to a map of your route, the *Travel Map*. It is an arcade game that is easy to pick up and hard to put down, and you can play it for free on itch.io.

## How we built it

We were a small team: four programmers and a 3D artist. Not everything fits into 48 hours, so we trimmed the original idea down to what made the game fun and polished that.
